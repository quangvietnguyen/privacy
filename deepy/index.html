Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 4, 2026 • Version 1.0 • iOS & Android
Key Principles
  • Offline-First Architecture: All your daily time blocks and mind sweep notes reside locally on your device.
  • Zero Advertising or Tracking: We do not track you across apps or websites, and we never sell your personal data.
  • Local System Notifications: Focus session alerts are scheduled locally on-device without remote push tokens.
  • User Ownership: You have the right to edit, export, or permanently delete your data at any time.

1. Overview

Deepy (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is a productivity application designed for time-blocking and focused deep work. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we process, the purposes for which it is collected, and your rights regarding your personal information when using the Deepy mobile application (the “App”).

2. Information We Collect

We adhere to strict data minimization. We only collect information essential to providing core application functionality:

Category Data Types Purpose
Account User ID, Email address, Name (via Apple or Google Sign-In) Authentication & account recovery
User Content Time blocks, Mind Sweep items, Categories, Durations, Completion status Core scheduling, analytics computation, and optional cloud sync
Diagnostics Anonymous crash telemetry, OS version, device model Performance monitoring and bug resolution

3. Purpose of Processing

We process collected information exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Rendering your daily schedule timeline and Mind Sweep inbox.
  • Computing focus duration and weekly consistency streak statistics.
  • Synchronizing your tasks across authenticated devices via Firebase Cloud Firestore.
  • Delivering on-device focus session start and completion alerts.
  • Authenticating your account securely.

4. Data Storage and Security

On-Device Storage: All tasks and local configurations are stored directly on your device using SQLite and platform-native secure storage (iOS Keychain and Android EncryptedSharedPreferences).

Cloud Infrastructure: For authenticated users who enable multi-device sync, data is processed through Google Cloud / Firebase Firestore. All network communications use TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, and database records are encrypted at rest using AES-256.

5. Third-Party Integrations

The App integrates exclusively with essential infrastructure providers:

  • Apple Sign-In (Apple Inc.): Authentication on iOS devices. Subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In & Firebase (Google LLC): Authentication and cloud database services. Subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.

We do not integrate any advertising SDKs, data broker networks, or third-party behavioral trackers.

6. Notifications

Notification permissions are requested solely to alert you when scheduled focus sessions begin or finish. Deepy uses local system notifications generated directly by the operating system. No notification contents or device tokens are transmitted to external push servers.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You hold complete control over your data:

  • In-App Item Deletion: You can delete any individual time block or mind sweep item at any time.
  • Account Deletion: You can request permanent deletion of your account and cloud records directly within the App or by contacting our support email. Cloud records will be permanently purged within 30 days of request.
  • App Removal: Deleting the App removes all local databases and cached credentials from your device.

8. Children’s Privacy

Deepy does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected, we will take immediate steps to delete that data.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in our service or regulatory requirements. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
