Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 4, 2026 • Version 1.0 • Applicable to: Deepy for iOS & Android
Summary of Key Principles
  • Offline-First Storage: Your time blocks, focus sessions, and mind sweeps are stored locally on your device by default.
  • No Third-Party Advertising: We do not serve ads, track your behavior across other apps, or sell your personal data.
  • Local Notifications: Scheduled reminders execute strictly on-device without remote tracking payloads.
  • Complete User Control: You can export, modify, or permanently delete your account and associated cloud data at any time.

1. Overview

Deepy (“we”, “our”, or “us”) provides focus scheduling and time-blocking tools designed to facilitate deep work. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how it is processed, and your rights concerning your personal data when using the Deepy mobile application (the “App”).

2. Information We Collect

We adhere to strict data minimization principles. We only collect information essential to providing core application functionality:

Category Data Types Purpose
Account User ID, Email address, Name (via Apple or Google Sign-In) User authentication & cloud account recovery
User Content Time blocks, Mind Sweep entries, Categories, Deadlines, Completion status Core scheduling, analytics computation, and optional multi-device synchronization
Diagnostics Anonymous crash logs, OS version, device model Performance monitoring and troubleshooting

3. Purpose of Processing

We process collected data exclusively for the following operational needs:

  • Rendering your daily schedule timeline and Mind Sweep inbox.
  • Aggregating focus duration and weekly consistency streak metrics.
  • Synchronizing user data across authenticated devices via Firebase Cloud Firestore.
  • Delivering on-device time block start and completion alerts.
  • Authenticating accounts and preventing unauthorized access.

4. Data Storage and Security

Local Persistence: All tasks and local settings are persisted on-device using SQLite and platform-level secure storage (Keychain / EncryptedSharedPreferences).

Cloud Infrastructure: When user accounts are linked, data synchronization is handled through Google Cloud / Firebase Firestore. All network communications use TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, and stored database assets are encrypted at rest using AES-256.

5. Third-Party Integrations

The App integrates solely with infrastructure partners necessary for authentication and persistence:

  • Apple Sign-In (Apple Inc.): Authentication on iOS devices. Subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In & Firebase (Google LLC): Authentication and cloud database services. Subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.

We do not integrate third-party ad networks, tracking SDKs, or data broker frameworks.

6. Notifications

Notification permissions are requested to alert you when scheduled time blocks begin or end. Deepy uses local system notifications generated directly by the operating system. No notification contents or device tokens are transmitted to external push notification servers.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You hold full ownership over your information:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete any individual time block or mind sweep item at any time.
  • Account Deletion: You can request immediate and permanent deletion of your account and cloud records directly inside the app or by emailing our support address. All cloud records will be purged within 30 days of request.
  • App Uninstallation: Deleting the app removes all local databases and cached credentials from your device.

8. Children’s Privacy

Deepy does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected, we will take immediate steps to delete that data.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in our service or regulatory updates. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
