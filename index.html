1. Introduction

Welcome to Deepy ("we", "our", or "us"). Deepy is a productivity application designed to help you organize your daily time blocks, capture distractions via Mind Sweep, and achieve deep, focused work.

We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, where it is stored, and your rights regarding your data when you use the Deepy mobile application (the "App").

2. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver core productivity and cross-device sync features:

  • Account Information: When you optionally create an account or sign in using Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In, we receive basic profile identifiers (such as your user ID, display name, and email address).
  • Productivity & Task Data: The time blocks, mind sweep items, completion statuses, category tags (Deep vs. Shallow), custom duration goals, and deadlines you create within the App.
  • Device & Technical Diagnostics: Standard technical information such as operating system version and anonymous crash reports to diagnose performance issues and ensure app stability.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To schedule and display your daily focus sessions and mind sweep inbox.
  • To calculate your focus metrics (such as daily deep work hours, streak consistency, and category ratios).
  • To synchronize your tasks and schedule across your devices when you are logged in.
  • To schedule local reminders on your device when a time block is starting or concluding.
  • To authenticate and maintain the security of your account.

4. Storage and Security

Offline-First Local Storage: All your tasks, settings, and schedules are stored locally on your device using SQLite and secure device storage. The app remains fully functional even without an internet connection.

Cloud Synchronization (Firebase): When you sign in, your data is securely synchronized with Firebase Cloud Firestore (hosted on Google Cloud Platform). All data in transit is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS), and all data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 encryption.

5. Third-Party Services

We partner only with reputable infrastructure providers essential for app operation:

  • Apple Sign-In: For private and secure authentication on iOS devices.
  • Google Sign-In: For secure Google authentication.
  • Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore: For user authentication and cloud data persistence.

We do not integrate any third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing trackers.

6. Notifications

Deepy requests permission to send notifications solely to alert you when your scheduled focus sessions begin or end. These are local notifications generated and scheduled directly by your device's operating system without sending payload data to external servers.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You retain complete ownership and control over your data:

  • In-App Task Deletion: You can edit or delete individual mind sweep items and schedule blocks at any time.
  • Account Deletion: You can request full account and data deletion at any time directly through the app settings or by contacting us at the email below. Upon request, all associated cloud records are permanently purged.
  • Local Data Removal: Uninstalling the App removes all locally cached SQLite databases and secure store records from your device.

8. Children's Privacy

Deepy is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any updates.
